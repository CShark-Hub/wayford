## How it works

`packages/web/src/components/ui/tooltip.tsx` exports one component, `Hint({ label, hint })`: a 32 px ghost `Button` (`size="icon-sm"`, `type="button"`) holding a lucide `Info` icon (20 px, stroke 1.75, `aria-hidden`), named by `label`, and shadcn's Tooltip content beside it holding `hint`. It wraps its own `Tooltip.Provider` (`delayDuration` 0), so any render works without an app-level provider, as web's `renderToStaticMarkup` tests need. The hint's words are also kept in a `hidden` span beside the button, which the button's `aria-describedby` names, so the button is described whether the bubble is open or not: a screen reader cursor that moves without moving focus (VoiceOver on iOS, TalkBack, NVDA browse mode) reads it too. Radix sets its own `aria-describedby` only while open; the prop overrides it (`dist/index.mjs:186–188`).

Radix opens a tooltip on hover and on keyboard focus, keeps it open while the pointer is on it (hoverable content), and closes it on Esc, blur, a press elsewhere, the pointer leaving, and any scroll of an ancestor of the trigger, the window included (`:316–326`). It never opens on a tap: its trigger ignores touch `pointermove`, and closes on `pointerdown` and on `click` (`:190–212`). So `Hint` holds the open state itself:

- **A click** (`event.preventDefault()`, which skips Radix's own close): from a touch it toggles from the state recorded at `pointerdown`, so a tap opens and a second tap closes; from the keyboard (`detail === 0`), a mouse or a pen it opens and never closes, so Enter, Space or a click on a button that focus or hover already opened keeps the hint (branch review).
- **While the trigger has focus, or a primary mouse or pen press is on it** (until its pointerup or pointercancel), a close Radix asks for is dropped, unless Esc asked (the content's `onEscapeKeyDown` sets a flag) or the trigger is losing focus (during `blur` the active element is no longer the trigger). So a Tab that scrolls an off-screen trigger into view, a wheel scroll, the pointer leaving, or the press itself cannot close a hint that focus or a press holds open (WCAG 2.2 1.4.13, Persistent). Esc, Tab away, a press elsewhere (which moves focus) and a second tap still close it.
- **Another hint opening** closes it, even one focus holds: while open, `Hint` listens for the `tooltip.open` event Radix sends on the document when a tooltip opens, and a click that opens a hint sends it too, since that open does not go through Radix. One hint is open at a time (branch review).

Measured in Chromium before this plan (throwaway probes, deleted): hover opens, the pointer can move onto the content, leaving closes; Tab opens, Esc closes with focus kept; a tap opens and stays open after the lift, a tap elsewhere closes, a second tap closes; axe with `AXE_WCAG_TAGS` finds nothing, open or closed. The plan review's probe found the scroll close and the keyboard toggle, and the branch review found a mouse click closing a hovered hint and two hints open at once; the rules above are their fixes.

The content is drawn from tokens: `surface`, a `line` hairline, `radius-sm`, `shadow-pop` (`docs/DESIGN.md`: popovers only), `body` in `ink` with `text-pretty`, padding `space-2`/`space-3`, `z-(--z-overlay)` and `collisionPadding={16}` as the persona HoverCard has. Its width is at most 20 rem and never more than Popper leaves (`--radix-tooltip-content-available-width`), so it reflows at 320 px (1.4.10). No arrow, and none of shadcn's `animate-*` classes: DESIGN's Motion allows colour and opacity only, and an exit animation would keep a closed tooltip mounted.

**Findings columns** (`report/findings.tsx`). `GROUPS` gains each group's `hint`. Each group's heading row becomes a `<header>` holding the `<h2>` (glyph, name, count and the three words, unchanged, `flex-1` so the words stay right) and, after it, `Hint` labelled `Explain Measurement`, `Explain Deterministic` or `Explain Hypothesis`. The rule and the padding move from the `<h2>` to the `<header>`. Outside the `<h2>`, the button's name stays out of the heading's, which is also the section's `aria-labelledby` and what every browser test waits on (`role=heading[name=/^Measurement/]`). A `<header>` in a section maps to no landmark. With a persona picked, the row holds "Deterministic 1 of 2 the filter, in code" plus the button in a 395 px column; if it cannot hold one line, the `<h2>` wraps (`flex-wrap`) and the three words drop under the name whole; the count and the words never break inside (branch review: without it they broke mid-phrase at 1024 px and with two-digit counts).

**Cast table** (`report/cast-table.tsx`). The "route open" and "got through" `<th>`s keep their `<p>` (glyph and label) and add `Hint` after the label, labelled `Explain route open` and `Explain got through`. Each `<th>` takes `aria-labelledby` pointing at a span holding its label (ids from `useId`), so the column header is still named "route open", not "route open Explain route open". The header row grows from 40 to about 48 px; the ten-seed fit test has about 70 px to spare.

**Copy** (fact-checked by the review against io):

| Where | Hint |
|---|---|
| Measurement | Measured by axe-core, an accessibility scanner, on pages the runs stood on, each loaded afresh at desktop size. It is about the page, not a persona, so it stays in view whichever persona is picked. What to change is the Analyst's. |
| Deterministic | Decided in code by the filter, from the persona's limits: what did not reach the model. The same seed gives the same decision. What to change is the Analyst's. |
| Hypothesis | Written by the Analyst, a model, from what the agent did in the runs. A reading worth checking, not a measured fact. |
| route open | Seeds where at least one route to the goal stayed open, out of the persona's seeds. Deterministic: the filter decides it in code, from the persona's limits and the seed, with no model. |
| got through | Runs that reached the goal, out of those that did not error. Code checks the goal; the way there is the agent's and varies, so read it as a hypothesis. |
