Part 1 gave the stage back the URL row's height: beat 2's frame went from 450 to 663 px wide, and "Order archive" from 5.6 to 8.3 px. This part draws the page at a legible scale.

Measured on `main` `c2219ac` at 1280 × 720, beat 2 (Vera on `/deliveries`):

- The stage is 932 × 390 px and the frame is height-bound at 0.52.
- The measured page has no margin to crop: its elements run from x 24 to 1256 and from y 12 to past 720 (the footer).
- So the whole page cannot be drawn legibly on this screen: 0.75 (12 px text) needs 960 × 540, more than the stage in both directions.

The user picked a zoom toggle (2026-10-11): Fit stays the default, Zoom draws the page at its measured size and the stage scrolls.

Acceptance, this part:

1. With zoom on, beat 2's "Order archive" is drawn at its measured 16 px, in both views. Scrolled to it, it lies inside the stage, and nothing scrolls on the document.
2. The zoom is in the URL (`zoom=true`). A persona link, a page link and the URL form keep it. A slider move, a view switch or a persona switch keeps the stage's scroll position on the same page.
3. The default, fit, is unchanged: part 1's beat 2 test still passes.
4. The scrolling stage can be reached from the keyboard, and the toggle says its state (`aria-pressed`).

## Decision log

| Question | Taken | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How to make the page legible | A zoom toggle, fit by default, full size (scale 1) in a scrolling stage (user, 2026-10-11) | The whole page cannot be legible on this stage. The other options were a compact readout strip (~10.4 px, the whole page) and width-fit with a scrolling stage (~11.2 px). Full size is the only one that reads from across a room, at the cost of one scroll before the beat |
| Full size or width-fit when zoomed | Full size, `--px` = 1 px | Text at its measured size, 16 px for "Order archive". Width-fit gains only 8.3 → 11.2 px |
| Where the scroll position lives | In the screen, per page, in memory | A view or persona switch remounts the page (it is keyed by scene), and beat 2 flips to evidence for a second. Losing the scroll there would lose "Order archive". Not in the URL: a pixel offset is not a link anyone shares |
| Where zoom lives | The URL, `zoom=true`, absent when off | It is a view setting, as the views are. A rehearsed link opens zoomed, and Back and reload keep it |
| Zoom in the scene key | Left out | Zoom changes no element's look, so toggling it has nothing to animate. Remounting would only lose the scroll |
| The scroller | An inner element over the measuring surface, not the surface itself | The surface's corner ticks are an absolutely placed `::before`, which would scroll away inside a scrolling surface. The page passes over them as it scrolls (review) |
| Centring an oversized frame | `place-items-center`, as `MEASURING_SURFACE` has | The grid's auto track grows to the 1282 px frame, which already keeps its edges in the scroll's reach; plain and safe centring measured the same, so safe centring was cut (second review) |
| The scroller's focus ring | Drawn 4 px inside (`focus-visible:-outline-offset-4`) (review) | The global ring sits 2 px outside, and the surface, which clips, hid all of it |
| The scroll's key | The measured page: device and page (review) | The measurement is keyed by both, so Milo's mobile `/deliveries` keeps its own scroll |
| The demo's beat 2 | Set up before beat 1: Full size on, scroll to "Order archive", Full size off. In beat 2, one press opens the stage at the link (review) | Beat 1 keeps the whole page fitted, and beat 2 costs one press, not a scroll |
| The Illustration view | The toggle is disabled there | The illustration is a picture, which zoom does not draw. Hiding the toggle would move the View control |
