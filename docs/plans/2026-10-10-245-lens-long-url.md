## Decision log

| Question | Taken | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Break the URL anywhere, as the issue proposes, or cut it short | Cut it short on one line, with an ellipsis, the whole URL in its `title` and its text | Wrapping stops the sideways scroll, but every extra line comes out of the frame: the issue's URL already costs it 128 px of width, and a 2000-character query string leaves a 2 × 2 frame over a caption 422 px tall. Cut short, the frame is 663 × 374 at every length tried, up to 2000 characters, in both views |
| Which part of the line gives way | The URL alone; `Live:` and ` on <device>, <opened>.` never shrink | The device and how the page was opened are the line's claim about the measurement (DESIGN, "Claim less"), and they are short. The URL's start, its origin and path, is what tells pages apart, so the ellipsis goes at its end |
| Where the whole URL stays | In the span's text, read by screen readers and copied with the line, and in its `title` on hover | The URL row (`Open a URL`) shows the whole URL too, in its field |
| How to keep the line's spaces in a flex row | `whitespace-pre` on the row, and the tail as one string | A flex row makes each text run an item of its own, and a run's edge space would otherwise collapse |
| The other lines that quote the URL (review at `81b2fc9`) | Measuring line and io's messages, in the axe card and the fault banner, break anywhere; the axe card's "scanning" and "did not run on" lines are cut short, whole in their `title` | On the issue's URL these ran the document 1490 to 2064 px wide and pushed **Try again** out of the window.
