Order: 1, then 2, 3 and 4 in parallel, then 5; 6 runs beside 2 to 5 (docs and one fixture, files disjoint); then 7, 8 and 9 in parallel; then 10. No step adds a dependency or touches `pnpm-lock.yaml`.

Notes:
- Step 3 needs step 1's types (`PopupKind`, `hasPopup`, `controls`), so it starts after 1 although it runs beside 2 and 4.
- Step 5 changes `perceive`'s signature; until step 7 lands, `packages/web` does not typecheck. Steps 7 to 9 start together right after 5, and the build's typecheck is judged after 7.
- Step 6's RULES text must state exactly what steps 2, 3 and 5 implement; step 2's worked values and step 8's route-table parse are the checks that it does.
- Each step's agent works only in its own files and reports a blocker instead of working around it. The RULES-frozen rule and the determinism rules (`docs/CONVENTIONS.md`) bind every step.
- The browser test files share Chromium with whatever else runs: a 5 s timeout on a collect-heavy test is first rerun alone (memory of the Windows load flake).
